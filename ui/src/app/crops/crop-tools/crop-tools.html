How to draw Drag on the crop to draw a {{ drawing() }}. Tap shapes to select them. Drag a box's corner (a 3D box's every corner) to move it on its own, for a box seen in perspective; a pill's or an oval's corner resizes it (an oval fits a sphere the camera stretches). A polygon's Sides set how many it has; Uniform keeps it regular (a corner scales it evenly, a side stretches it), Each vertex gives every vertex its own handle, and Uniform again fits the nearest regular polygon to them. A square on a side moves that side (Mirror or Alt: its opposite side too), the round handle above turns it. A 3D shape has a square on each face (hollow: a face turned away) and a ringed handle below: drag it to tumble the shape in 3D, or use the 3D turn buttons. Its faces toward you are shaded and its hidden edges dashed. The wheel over the selected shapes resizes them, elsewhere it zooms. Shift while drawing or resizing makes a perfect circle or square. Tap the wall for a tiny target.
