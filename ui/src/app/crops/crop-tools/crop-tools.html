How to draw Drag on the crop to draw a {{ drawing() }}. Tap shapes to select them. Drag a box's corner (a 3D box's every corner) to move it on its own, for a box seen in perspective; a pill's corner resizes it. A square on a side moves that side (Mirror or Alt: its opposite side too), the round handle above turns it. A 3D shape has a square on each face (hollow: a face turned away) and a ringed handle below: drag it to tumble the shape in 3D, or use the 3D turn buttons. Its faces toward you are shaded and its hidden edges dashed. The wheel over the selected shapes resizes them, elsewhere it zooms. Shift while drawing or resizing makes a perfect circle or square. Tap the wall for a tiny target.
