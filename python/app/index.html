Runs with a stats file (challenge mode) get the full review. Without one, the review reads KovaaK's session HUD when it is on screen, and otherwise finds the kills in the video alone.

Drag on the video to cover anything that is not the game: a webcam, an overlay, a stream's text. The review ignores targets found there. Drag an area to move it, its edge or corner to resize it; click it to say what it is, or to remove it. Where areas overlap, click again for the one underneath.
